# Ability Icons Round 13

## Raspberry

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: production Minecraft mod ability icon source, intended for nearest-neighbor reduction to a 64x64 RGBA item texture and still readable at 32x32

Primary request: Create an isolated pixel-art sprite of exactly two ripe raspberries touching and slightly overlapping as one compact cluster.

Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid `#00ffff` chroma-key background for later removal; one uniform color only, no shadows, gradients, texture, floor, or lighting variation; do not use `#00ffff` anywhere in the subject.

Subject: exactly two raspberries. One berry sits in the lower-left foreground and the other is slightly higher and to the right, but they are almost beside one another rather than forming a steep diagonal. Their bodies physically touch and overlap slightly; there must be no empty gap between them. Together they occupy about 70 percent of the square canvas. Each berry has large simplified drupelet clusters, dark berry-red shadows, saturated raspberry-red midtones, restrained pink highlights, and one or two hard pure-white shine pixels on its upper-left. On top of each berry is a green six-point star-shaped calyx plus exactly one narrow green stem or leaf pointing upward.

Style/medium: authentic hand-placed Minecraft-style pixel art, chunky deliberate square pixel clusters, hard crisp edges, limited palette, binary-looking silhouette, no fine micro-detail.

Composition/framing: centered compact pair with generous transparent-intent padding and no clipping; lower-left berry slightly in front; upper-right berry only modestly raised.

Constraints: exactly two berries, both touching; strong unified silhouette; no text, watermark, frame, badge, status symbol, face, basket, plate, scene, cast shadow, glow, blur, antialiasing, smooth vector curves, or extra fruit. The final subject must remain recognizable when reduced to 32x32.

## Quick Punch

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: production Minecraft mod ability icon source, intended for nearest-neighbor reduction to a 64x64 RGBA item texture and still readable at 32x32

Primary request: Create an isolated pixel-art sprite of exactly one generic human forearm ending in one clenched punch, with a compact yellow cartoon impact burst behind the fist.

Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid `#00ffff` chroma-key background for later removal; one uniform color only, no shadows, gradients, texture, floor, or lighting variation; do not use `#00ffff` anywhere in the subject.

Subject: exactly one bare human fist and visible bare forearm. The arm enters from the bottom-right corner and punches diagonally toward the upper-left. The fist is the dominant focal point and larger than the end of the forearm. Fingers are chunky and mostly squared, with no fingernails; use three simple dark lines to separate the curled fingers and a strong dark outline around the hand. The thumb crosses the upper side of the clenched fist and is clearly visible near the top-right portion of the fist. Use a limited warm skin palette with dark colored shadows, midtone, blocky highlights, and a small hard near-white shine along the upper arm. Behind the fist only, add a small pure-yellow comic impact shape made of five to seven tapered triangular spikes. The spikes peek outward around the fist but have no circular center and do not resemble fire or a full explosion. The hand and arm remain the main subject.

Style/medium: authentic hand-placed Minecraft-style pixel art, chunky deliberate square pixel clusters, hard crisp edges, limited palette, strong silhouette, no fine micro-detail.

Composition/framing: diagonal bottom-right to upper-left; subject occupies roughly 75 percent of the canvas with generous padding and no clipping.

Constraints: exactly one arm and exactly one fist; generic skin only, no glove, sleeve, jewelry, character costume, fingernails, secondary fists, echo arms, stun stars, lightning, text, watermark, frame, badge, scene, cast shadow, glow, blur, antialiasing, smooth vector curves, or soft haze. Final subject must remain recognizable at 32x32.

The same processed artwork is exported for `quick_punch`, `quick_punch_raw`, and `quick_punch_stun`.

## Punchies

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: production Minecraft mod ability icon source, intended for nearest-neighbor reduction to a 64x64 RGBA item texture and still readable at 32x32

Primary request: Create an isolated Minecraft-style pixel-art sprite for Billy Numerous's four-hit Punchies ability: exactly four fully costumed red-and-black punching arms, consisting of one dominant main arm and exactly three smaller subordinate echo arms.

Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid `#00ffff` chroma-key background for later removal; one uniform color only, no shadows, gradients, texture, floor, or lighting variation; do not use `#00ffff` anywhere in the subject.

Subject: all four arms punch diagonally from the bottom-right toward the upper-left. They begin from slightly different lower-right positions and fan outward at small differing angles, almost parallel rather than radial. The main arm is in front, darkest, largest, and most detailed. Its clenched fist is deliberately oversized and chunkier than its forearm, with squared blocky knuckles, a visible top thumb shape, and minimal dark finger-separation lines. The hand, wrist, and forearm are completely covered by Billy Numerous's fitted red costume: absolutely no exposed skin anywhere. A flat black costume stripe crosses the wrist/forearm and follows the suit surface; it is not a raised bracelet. Add a small hard pale highlight on the red main arm. The three echo arms are progressively smaller and lower-contrast, using darker/desaturated red and black while remaining opaque; simplify their finger lines but keep each recognizable as a fully covered red fist and arm. No yellow impact burst—the repeated costumed arms communicate the multi-hit attack.

Style/medium: authentic hand-placed Minecraft-style pixel art, chunky deliberate square pixel clusters, hard crisp edges, limited red/black palette, strong unified silhouette, no fine micro-detail.

Composition/framing: one main diagonal arm dominates the center from lower-right to upper-left; three echoes remain visibly distinct behind it and fan within a narrow angle; the complete group occupies roughly 80 percent of the canvas with padding and no clipping.

Constraints: exactly four total arms and four total fists; every hand and arm fully covered by the red-and-black striped suit; no exposed skin, fingernails, bare fingers, jewelry, raised wristband, yellow burst, fire, lightning, text, watermark, frame, badge, scene, cast shadow, glow, blur, antialiasing, smooth vector curves, or soft haze. Final subject must remain readable at 32x32.
