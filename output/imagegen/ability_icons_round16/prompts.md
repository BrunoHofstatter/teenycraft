## Sonic Cannon

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: production Minecraft Forge mod ability icon source, intended for nearest-neighbor reduction to a 64x64 transparent RGBA item texture and still readable at 32x32

Primary request: Create Blue Beetle's Sonic Cannon ability icon as exactly one compact alien sonic cannon actively firing. The cannon enters from the lower-right and aims diagonally toward the upper-left, but the physical cannon itself occupies only about one-third of the full square composition; it must not be a long rifle or long barrel. It has a short cylindrical dark-but-saturated blue body with two restrained pale blue-white stripes. From the muzzle, show a large sonic discharge traveling toward the upper-left across much of the remaining composition, built from exactly three separated expanding crescent wave fronts in pale cyan and blue-white. The waves must read as sound pulses, not a continuous laser or explosion.

Constraints: exactly one cannon, exactly one mounted antenna stick with one ball, exactly three sonic wave fronts; strong silhouette; background is one uniform `#ff00ff` color with no shadows, gradients, texture, floor, reflection, or lighting variation; do not use magenta anywhere in the subject; no cast shadow; no contact shadow; no text; no watermark; no frame; no character; no hands; no shield symbol
