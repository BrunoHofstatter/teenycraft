# Teeny Craft ability icon prompts — round 2

Generation mode: built-in image generation. Each source used a flat `#ff00ff` chroma background, which was removed locally before nearest-neighbor reduction to 32x32.

## Axe to Grind

Create Sinestro's entirely yellow hard-light double-headed battle axe swinging diagonally like a Minecraft tool, from bottom-left to top-right. Both broad symmetrical heads are slightly less than half-circles, with curved concave inner cuts rather than straight cuts. Include one restrained curved yellow swing trail. Use chunky Minecraft pixel art, a yellow/lemon/gold/amber palette, crisp edges, and no wood or metal colors.

## Bang

Create one round matte-black cartoon bomb with a short dark fuse curving toward the upper-right. Paint one full vivid-red heart on the middle of the bomb, recognizable but with slightly irregular paint-stain edges. End the fuse with a tiny orange-yellow ember. Use chunky Minecraft pixel art, crisp edges, and a limited charcoal/red palette.

## Bat Mine

Create one thin symmetrical black bat-shaped batarang with wide pointed wings, a center bat-head notch, sharp outer tips, subtle charcoal highlights only along the outer wings, and one small vivid-red warning light at its exact center. Tilt it slightly from bottom-left toward top-right. Use chunky Minecraft pixel art and preserve a very clear thin silhouette.

## Bat Mine button

Create one oversized round bright-red mechanical push button viewed from a slight three-quarter top-down angle. Give it a simple dark-red rim and only a small near-black base underneath. The red cap should dominate and look immediately pressable. Use chunky Minecraft pixel art and a very simple silhouette.

## Shared constraints

Isolated sprite with generous padding; readable at 32x32; no character, hand, text, icon frame, UI circle, blur, gradients, antialiasing, cast shadow, or watermark. Perfectly uniform `#ff00ff` background with no magenta inside the subject.
