# Ability Icon Round 20 Prompts

Built-in image generation was used once for each distinct texture. Each source
was generated on a flat chroma background, processed with the installed
ImageGen chroma-removal helper, reduced to 64x64 with nearest-neighbor
sampling, and checked again at 32x32.

## Plasma Shot

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production Minecraft mod ability icon source, intended for reduction to a 64x64 transparent PNG and readability at 32x32
Primary request: Plasma Shot for Cyborg — show the ability happening as exactly one compact futuristic forearm cannon firing exactly one detached plasma ball
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background for local background removal; one uniform color, no shadows, gradients, texture, reflections, floor plane, or lighting variation
Style/medium: authentic hand-placed Minecraft-style pixel art; deliberate square pixel clusters, hard stair-stepped edges, strong silhouette, limited palette, deliberately chunky and low-detail rather than a smooth illustration
Composition/framing: square canvas; aimed diagonal exactly from bottom-right toward upper-left at about 45 degrees; the entire cannon itself occupies only about 50% of the canvas, centered slightly toward the bottom-right; generous transparent-intent space around it; one plasma ball sits near the upper-left end with a small clear gap after the muzzle
Subject details: the cannon is not a straight cylinder—it is thickest through the middle and gradually tapers thinner toward both endpoints. Divide the cannon length into a roughly 70% rear/main section and a 30% smaller front shooting section. The 30% front section nearest the upper-left muzzle is light gray. Separate it from the main section with a very narrow open mechanical gap crossed by only one or two tiny dark connector wires. Split the 70% main section lengthwise into two clean halves: one Cyborg light cyan-blue half and one white/pale-gray half. Use dark blue-gray outline and shadows. The detached plasma ball has exactly two principal cyan shades: a deeper cyan outline and a pale cyan interior, hard-edged and roundish, no soft glow haze.
Color palette: white, pale gray, medium light cyan-blue, deeper cyan, dark blue-gray; #ff00ff only in the background
Constraints: exactly one cannon and exactly one plasma ball; no arm, hand, person, face, text, watermark, frame, UI border, circular background, scene, impact explosion, large energy trail, micro-circuit detail, blur, antialiasing, smooth vector curves, soft glow, cast shadow, or extra objects; keep the subject separated from the background with crisp edges and padding; do not use #ff00ff anywhere in the subject
```

## Plasma Shot focused revision

This revision replaces the initial Plasma Shot direction while retaining
`ability_plasma_shot_previous.png` and its previous preview/QA image for
comparison.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: focused revision of a production Minecraft mod ability icon source, intended for reduction to a 64x64 transparent PNG and readability at 32x32
Primary request: Regenerate Plasma Shot for Cyborg as exactly one compact futuristic forearm cannon firing exactly one substantially larger, fast-moving plasma projectile
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background for local removal; one uniform color with no shadows, gradients, texture, reflections, floor plane, or lighting variation
Style/medium: authentic hand-placed Minecraft-style pixel art; deliberate large square pixel clusters, crisp stair-stepped edges, strong silhouette, limited palette, chunky low detail, no smooth illustration
Composition/framing: square canvas; aimed diagonal from bottom-right toward upper-left at about 45 degrees. Push the cannon distinctly close to the bottom-right corner with only a narrow safe margin around its rear end. The cannon itself should occupy about half the canvas, leaving the upper-left area for the projectile. Place the projectile farther up-left beyond the muzzle with a clean separation.
Cannon invariants: not a straight cylinder; thickest through its middle and tapering gradually at both endpoints. Keep the roughly 70% rear/main section and 30% smaller light-gray front shooting section. Separate them with a very narrow open mechanical gap crossed by only one or two tiny dark connector wires. Split the 70% main section lengthwise into one Cyborg light-cyan half and one white/pale-gray half, with dark blue-gray outline and shadows.
Projectile revision: make the plasma projectile noticeably larger than before, roughly one and a half times the cannon's maximum thickness. It must not be a plain circle. Give it a rounded, full leading edge toward the upper-left, but a jagged tapered trailing edge toward the lower-right/cannon. Build that trailing edge from exactly three small triangular pixel spikes of unequal length, like a compact comet or fast energy bolt. The projectile should still read primarily as one bright plasma ball, not a long beam. Use a deeper cyan outline and pale cyan interior with hard edges and no soft glow haze.
Color palette: white, pale gray, Cyborg light cyan-blue, deeper cyan, dark blue-gray; #ff00ff only in the background
Constraints: exactly one cannon and exactly one plasma projectile; no arm, hand, character, face, text, watermark, frame, UI border, circular background, scene, impact explosion, long energy trail, separate particles, micro-circuit detail, blur, antialiasing, smooth vector curves, soft glow, cast shadow, or extra objects; keep all subject pixels separated from the chroma background with crisp edges; do not use #ff00ff anywhere in the subject
```
