# Ability Icons Round 24

All three source images were generated separately with the built-in image
generation path. The supplied Teeny Titans images were used as concept
references rather than edit targets. Each source used a flat magenta
chroma-key background for local removal and final transparent export.

## Nature's Wrath

Create a production Minecraft Forge mod ability icon source intended for
nearest-neighbor reduction to a 64x64 transparent RGBA texture. Use the
supplied green-spike badge only as a concept reference; do not copy its
circular badge, background, or exact artwork.

Create an isolated, authentic hand-placed Minecraft-style pixel-art action
sprite with exactly three thorny plant spikes erupting from one compact base
of blocky leaves. Use exactly one large dominant conical spike rising from the
lower center and leaning slightly toward the upper right. Place exactly two
clearly smaller subordinate spikes behind it, one on each side at roughly half
height. The three-spike eruption communicates group damage.

Use a limited palette of very dark forest-green outline, deep emerald shadows,
saturated leaf-green midtones, and sparse lime and mint highlights. Favor
chunky square clusters, hard stepped edges, and a strong triangular silhouette
that remains readable at 32x32. Avoid fine leaf veins and micro-detail.

Place the sprite on a perfectly flat solid `#ff00ff` chroma-key background.
Do not use magenta in the subject. No character, text, watermark, frame,
golden ring, circular UI badge, pink inner field, scene, dirt cloud, cast
shadow, glow haze, antialiasing, blur, or smooth vector curves.

## Root

Create a production Minecraft Forge mod ability icon source intended for
nearest-neighbor reduction to a 64x64 transparent RGBA texture. Use the
generated Nature's Wrath source as a strict palette reference. Use the
supplied lock badge only as a concept reference; do not copy its circular
badge, background, red burst, or exact artwork.

Create an isolated, authentic hand-placed Minecraft-style pixel-art action
sprite with exactly one large pale mint-green padlock slightly below center.
Give it a broad shackle and one bold near-black forest-green keyhole. Behind
the lock, place exactly two thick thorny roots crossing diagonally into a clean
X, with their pointed tips extending above and to the sides. Add at most one
restrained vine segment wrapping around a lower corner without covering the
keyhole. The lock must be the dominant subject and communicate that the
opponent is prevented from swapping.

Match Nature's Wrath's dark forest-green, emerald, leaf-green, lime, and mint
family. Use hard stepped edges, chunky square clusters, and a broad readable
silhouette that survives reduction to 32x32.

Place the sprite on a perfectly flat solid `#ff00ff` chroma-key background.
Do not use magenta in the subject. No character, text, watermark, frame,
circular badge, red fragments, scene, cast shadow, glow haze, antialiasing,
blur, smooth vector curves, tiny leaves, or noisy particles.

## Shockwave Stomp

Create a production Minecraft Forge mod ability icon source intended for
nearest-neighbor reduction to a 64x64 transparent RGBA texture. Use only the
bright inner shockwave pattern of the supplied badge as a concept reference.
Do not copy its golden circular frame, blue background, or exact artwork.

Create an isolated, authentic hand-placed Minecraft-style pixel-art action
sprite made only from chunky yellow shockwave lines. Show exactly three broad,
flattened concentric oval wave bands expanding horizontally from a central
impact point in the lower-middle. From the innermost ring, raise one short,
wide jagged impact wedge shaped like a blunt ground-impact burst rather than a
tall thin lightning bolt. Keep the widest outer band dominant and clearly
separate each ring with open transparent space.

Use a limited palette of dark warm amber stepped edging, saturated
golden-yellow bodies, and sparse pale-lemon highlights. Use thick square pixel
clusters and hard stepped edges so the open line-only symbol remains readable
at 32x32.

Place the sprite on a perfectly flat solid `#ff00ff` chroma-key background.
Do not use magenta in the subject. No foot, leg, character, ground disc,
rocks, dust cloud, outside sparks, text, watermark, frame, golden circle, blue
fill, circular UI badge, scene, cast shadow, glow haze, antialiasing, blur, or
smooth vector curves.
