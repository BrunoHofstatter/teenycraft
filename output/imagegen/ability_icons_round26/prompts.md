# Ability Icons Round 26

All three source images were generated separately with the built-in image
generation path. The supplied Teeny Titans images were used as concept and
composition references rather than edit targets. Flat chroma-key backgrounds
were removed locally before nearest-neighbor production export.

This round deliberately preserves more medium-fine detail at 64x64. The 32x32
exports are QA views rather than the primary design target.

## Shuriken

Create exactly one elegant four-point metal shuriken as an isolated production
Minecraft Forge mod ability-item sprite. Use the supplied icon only as a
reference for the four-point concept and orange center detail; do not copy its
circular UI frame or exact artwork.

Show the shuriken at a convincing three-quarter throwing angle rather than
perfectly face-on: rotate it diagonally, modestly foreshorten it, and make the
nearest blade slightly larger and brighter than the narrower, darker far
blade. Use four tapered cool blue-gray steel blades, dark navy edge pixels,
pale steel highlights, an orange-red circular hub ring, and a clearly open
central hole. Add only two or three restrained trailing motion pixels.

Use authentic hand-placed Minecraft-style pixel art with crisp hard edges,
deliberate square clusters, a limited but nuanced palette, medium detail, and
material shading. Use a perfectly flat `#ff00ff` chroma background. Exactly one
shuriken; no hand, character, gold circle, orange panel, UI frame, text,
watermark, cast shadow, glow haze, blur, antialiasing, or extra props.

## Nuh Uh!

Create an isolated expressive production Minecraft Forge mod action sprite of
exactly one warm-tan human hand making a clear "no / nuh-uh" gesture with one
raised index finger wagging side to side. Use the supplied icon only as a
reference for the gesture, split lettering, and motion arcs; do not copy its
gold circle, background, or exact artwork.

The wrist enters from the right, the hand crosses the center, and the index
finger points upper-left. Include two or three curved pale-blue movement arcs.
Render the exact text `NUH` once on the upper-left and `UH!` once on the
lower-right. Use playful pixel lettering with white fill, medium cyan-blue
outline, and selective deep-blue shadow pixels. Let the words overlap the hand
slightly while remaining legible.

Use detailed Minecraft-style pixel art with crisp hard clusters, nuanced warm
skin shading, visible simplified knuckle structure, and an energetic comic
composition. Use a perfectly flat `#ff00ff` chroma background. No circular
frame, gold ring, background scene, shield, medical cross, cleanse badge,
character face, extra hand, extra text, watermark, shadow, glow haze, blur, or
antialiasing.

## Plasma Shot - Jinx

Create exactly one isolated diagonal production Minecraft Forge mod aimed
ability sprite of Jinx's pale gray-lilac hand firing exactly one charged plasma
shot. Use the supplied plasma and hand images as conceptual references only;
do not reproduce their circular frames or backgrounds.

Her dark aubergine sleeve and wrist enter from the bottom-right. The hand opens
and aims naturally toward the upper-left. Place one irregular hot-pink plasma
projectile beyond the fingertips, following an approximately 45-degree attack
line. Make it slightly oval with a jagged rear edge and short tail. Use a
magenta outer edge, hot-pink body, pale-pink highlight, and a few white-hot
pixels. Add two compact broken wake streaks between the palm and projectile,
but no second projectile. Keep a small gap between the fingertips and plasma.

Use detailed Minecraft-style pixel art with crisp hard edges, deliberate pixel
clusters, nuanced limited shading, and medium-fine detail. Use pale cool
gray-lilac skin, cool blue-gray shadow planes, a deep-purple outline, and a
dark plum sleeve. Use a perfectly flat `#00ff00` chroma background. Exactly one
hand, forearm, and projectile; no second hand or orb, character face or body,
purple circular frame, black disk, UI border, text, watermark, cast shadow,
glow haze, blur, antialiasing, or extra objects.
