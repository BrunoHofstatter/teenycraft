# Ability Icons Round 27

Built-in image generation was used once per distinct ability texture. Each
source was generated on a flat magenta chroma background, preserved unchanged,
processed to binary alpha, and reduced with nearest-neighbor sampling to the
64x64 production texture.

## Smoke Bomb

Reference role: the supplied Teeny Titans icon was used only for the tilted
can concept and general composition. Its circular UI background was excluded.

> Use case: stylized-concept
> Asset type: production Minecraft mod ability icon source for Teeny Craft,
> ability id smoke_bomb
> Input image: composition and concept reference only. Reinterpret it as an
> original Minecraft-style pixel-art sprite; do not copy its yellow/red
> circular UI frame or background.
> Primary request: exactly one dark metal smoke can tilted on the same
> lower-left to upper-right angle as the reference, with the nozzle toward the
> upper-right. The can should be neither tiny nor oversized: the can body
> occupies about half the square canvas height, with a large plume of smoke
> above it. Put a bold simple red X emblem on the front of the can, not a
> letter and no other text. The smoke consists of several overlapping rounded
> blocky puffs, visibly smoke rather than fire, colored deep crimson,
> vermilion, and restrained red-orange highlights. Make the can charcoal and
> blue-gray steel with crisp highlights.
> Style/medium: authentic hand-placed Minecraft pixel art, strong readable
> silhouette, deliberate square pixel clusters, hard edges, limited palette.
> Moderately detailed 96x96 pixel-art design intended for clean
> nearest-neighbor reduction to a final 64x64 Minecraft texture.
> Composition: isolated centered can-and-smoke event with generous
> transparent-intent padding, balanced so the can remains dominant and the
> smoke has room without touching the canvas edges.
> Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background, one
> uniform color with no shadows, gradients, texture, floor, lighting variation,
> or vignette. Do not use #ff00ff anywhere in the subject.
> Constraints: exactly one can; red X symbol only; opaque blocky smoke puffs;
> no character; no hand; no circular frame; no UI button; no scene; no cast
> shadow; no glow haze; no blur; no antialiasing; no smooth vector curves; no
> watermark; no text.

### Orange-smoke revision

The original generated source remained the edit target. The built-in image
editor supplied the warmer smoke palette, and the revised colors were
composited only over the original upper smoke pixels so the can remained
unchanged.

> Use case: precise-object-edit
> Asset type: production Minecraft mod ability icon source revision
> Input image: edit target.
> Primary request: change only the color palette of the smoke plume above the
> can, making it a little more orange. Shift the smoke from predominantly
> red/crimson to a warmer red-orange palette: dark burnt-orange shadows, strong
> orange-red midtones, and clear vivid orange highlights. Keep some restrained
> red in the deepest areas so it remains smoke and does not look like yellow
> fire.
> Strict invariants: preserve the smoke's exact shape, number of puffs, pixel
> clusters, placement, and size. Preserve the entire can pixel-for-pixel:
> identical silhouette, angle, dimensions, nozzle, metal colors, shading,
> highlights, and red X emblem. Preserve the perfectly flat solid magenta
> chroma background exactly. Do not add, remove, resize, redraw, or reposition
> anything. No yellow flames, no new glow, no text, no frame, no other changes.

## The Heal

rainbow-heart arrangement. Its circular UI frame and background were excluded.

> Use case: stylized-concept
> Asset type: production Minecraft mod ability icon source for Teeny Craft,
> ability id the_heal
> Input image: concept and arrangement reference only. Reinterpret it as
> original Minecraft-style pixel art; do not copy its gray/teal circular UI
> frame or background.
> Primary request: a centered healing emblem made of exactly one broad upward
> rainbow arch and exactly three red hearts resting along its upper curve. The
> middle heart sits slightly higher and is only modestly larger than the two
> side hearts, clearly communicating a three-member team heal. The rainbow has
> five thick, clean, clearly separated bands in red, warm yellow-orange, green,
> cyan-blue, and violet-purple. Place exactly five tiny pure-white pixel

> silhouette, deliberate square pixel clusters, hard edges, limited palette.

> Composition: broad centered emblem occupying about 80 percent of the canvas
> width, with the rainbow in the lower half and all three hearts fully readable
> above it; generous padding and no clipping. Hearts use saturated red, dark

> Constraints: exactly one rainbow arch, exactly three hearts, exactly five

## Cuteness

Reference role: the supplied Teeny Titans icon was used only for the
front-facing cat and oversized doll-eye concept. Its photograph, circular UI
frame, and background were excluded.

> Use case: stylized-concept
> Asset type: production Minecraft mod ability icon source for Teeny Craft,
> ability id cuteness
> Input image: concept and facial-expression reference only. Reinterpret it as
> an original cartoon Minecraft-style pixel-art cat face; do not copy the
> realistic photograph, gray/teal circular UI frame, or background.
> Primary request: exactly one front-facing cartoon tabby cat head with two
> pointed ears, rounded cheeks, and two deliberately oversized, nearly circular
> doll-like eyes dominating the face. Integrate the eyes naturally into the
> pixel-art face. Each eye has a pale cream outer ring, warm reddish-brown iris,
> very dark round pupil, and one crisp pure-white reflection. Add a tiny pink
> triangular nose, a minimal small mouth, and a few bold tabby markings on the
> forehead and cheeks.
> Style/medium: cute authentic hand-placed Minecraft pixel art, strong readable
> silhouette, deliberate square pixel clusters, hard edges, limited palette,
> expressive but not photorealistic. Moderately detailed 96x96 pixel-art design
> intended for clean nearest-neighbor reduction to a final 64x64 Minecraft
> texture.
> Composition: isolated symmetrical cat head centered in the square, ears and
> cheeks fully visible, head occupies about 75 to 80 percent of the canvas with
> generous padding. Warm cream and tan fur, medium brown stripes and colored
> shadows; no individual hair rendering.
> Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background, one
> uniform color with no shadows, gradients, texture, floor, lighting variation,
> or vignette. Do not use #ff00ff anywhere in the subject.
> Constraints: exactly one cat head and exactly two eyes; no body; no paws; no
> collar; no hearts; no sparkles; no circular frame; no scene; no cast shadow;
> no glow haze; no realism; no fur strands; no blur; no antialiasing; no smooth
> vector curves; no watermark; no text.
