the red/yellow zoning reference. Preserve the exact silhouette, position,
direction, rounded nose, jagged tail, negative space, and four speed streaks.
Change only the color construction.

fields, not a border: bright golden yellow forms the main forward teardrop and

The accepted revision explicitly removed that outline and exposed yellow at
the forward perimeter.
