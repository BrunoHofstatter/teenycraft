# Ability Icons Round 31

The built-in image-generation path produced three distinct large sources from
the supplied Teeny Titans references. Each source used a flat green chroma
background for local removal before nearest-neighbor export to 64x64 RGBA.
Quick Punch Stun and Mighty Punch intentionally share the exact same processed
artwork.

## `katana_slam`

Image 1 was the reference for the sword proportions, palette, guard, handle
bands, and small metallic collar above the guard. Generate exactly one sword,
not the crossed pair or circular badge. Place its handle at lower-left and its
broad, mostly straight silver blade toward upper-right in a Minecraft-tool
diagonal. Use a black handle and swept guard, two dark-red/orange handle bands,
a blocky silver-blue collar, blue-gray blade shading, and a charcoal outline.
Add exactly one extremely subtle broken pale-gray slash arc just in front of
the cutting edge, using only a few separated pixel clusters so it cannot read
as a second blade.

Authentic hand-placed Minecraft-style pixel art; moderately detailed
96x96-style design intended for nearest-neighbor reduction to 64x64. One
isolated subject on flat `#00ff00`; no second sword, character, hand, circular
badge, impact explosion, stun stars, text, watermark, shadow, glow, soft blur,
smooth curves, or antialiasing.

## `wing_ding`

Image 2 was the reference for the unusual batarang silhouette, dark body,
crimson edge accents, and motion treatment. Generate exactly one centered
Nightwing batarang, nearly upright with a subtle five-to-eight-degree left
lean. Preserve the long upper wing, pointed lower tail, angular ear-like inner
notches, and asymmetric aerodynamic profile. Use near-black, charcoal, and
restrained deep-purple highlights, with narrow crimson accents along the upper
wing, outer rim, and lower tail. Express speed through hard-edged stepped
red-and-charcoal pixel echoes around the wing, notches, and tail. Keep the
primary batarang much stronger than its echoes.

Authentic hand-placed Minecraft-style pixel art; moderately detailed
96x96-style design intended for nearest-neighbor reduction to 64x64. One
isolated subject on flat `#00ff00`; no extra projectile, character, circular
purple badge, target rings, text, watermark, shadow, glow, soft blur, smooth
curves, or antialiasing.

## `quick_punch_stun` and `mighty_punch`

Image 3 was the reference for the side-view fist, straight forearm, coral
palette, and yellow impact burst. Generate exactly one bare straight forearm
entering from lower-right and punching toward upper-left. Use a tall,
rectangular clenched fist with a stepped knuckle ridge, three simple dark
finger separations, a visible thumb folded across the fingers, and a narrowed
wrist. Use coral-salmon midtones, peach highlights, and dark red-brown outlines
and shadows. Behind the fist only, add one compact two-layer burst with seven
to nine broad golden-yellow spikes and a few smaller orange-red wedges.

Authentic hand-placed Minecraft-style pixel art; moderately detailed
96x96-style design intended for nearest-neighbor reduction to 64x64. One
isolated subject on flat `#00ff00`; no bent bicep, elbow ball, sleeve, glove,
jewelry, fingernails, extra fist, stun stars, lightning, fire, full explosion,
text, watermark, shadow, glow, blur, smooth curves, or antialiasing.
