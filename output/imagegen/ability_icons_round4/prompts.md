## Beatbox Heal

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: production Minecraft Forge mod ability icon, source sprite for a final 32x32 transparent texture

Primary request: Create the Beatbox Heal ability icon as a single cute animated radio that visibly represents healing. The radio is centered in the lower-middle. It has one squat rectangular body with a light-gray metal outer casing, a dark-gray front panel, and a near-black pixel outline. Put exactly two symmetrical dark-gray circular speakers on the front, one on the left and one on the right. Two thin straight metal-stick arms emerge from the sides and angle upward in a celebratory V; they have no hands. Two thin straight metal-stick legs emerge from below and point vertically downward; they have no feet. Float exactly one red-pink pixel heart above the radio with a small transparent gap, using a dark-red edge and one light-pink highlight.

Style and composition: Authentic hand-placed Minecraft-style pixel art with deliberately chunky square pixel clusters, hard stair-stepped edges, a limited palette, generous padding, and a silhouette that remains recognizable at 32x32 and 16x16.

Constraints: Exactly one radio, exactly two speakers, exactly two arms, exactly two legs, and exactly one heart. Keep the casing and speakers symmetrical. No hands, feet, face, antenna, knobs, equalizer, music notes, text, watermark, frame, circular UI plate, floor, cast shadow, reflection, gradients, blur, smooth vector curves, antialiasing, or glow haze. Perfectly flat `#00ff00` chroma background; no green in the sprite.

## Birdarang

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: production Minecraft Forge mod ability icon, source sprite for a final 32x32 transparent texture

Primary request: Create exactly one Birdarang, a sleek black technological boomerang shaped like a bird, shown from directly above. Its silhouette is made from two intersecting parts: an extremely thin, wide, shallow crescent forming two swept bird wings, curving downward so both outer wing tips point toward the bottom; and a very narrow vertical tapered ellipse or pointed lozenge crossing through the exact middle of the wing crescent at a perpendicular 90-degree angle. The central body is especially needle-thin at its upper tip. Put exactly one tiny crisp red technology light on that upper tip. It must not look like a normal V boomerang, bat logo, airplane, or literal feathered bird.

Style and composition: Authentic hand-placed Minecraft-style pixel art with hard stair-stepped edges, a predominantly black silhouette, restrained charcoal edge highlights, generous padding, and no diagonal rotation or motion trail.

Constraints: Exactly one Birdarang and exactly one red light. No feathers, eyes, beak details, separated wing pieces, Batman logo, yellow accents, characters, hands, extra projectiles, text, watermark, frame, circular UI plate, floor, cast shadow, reflection, gradients, blur, smooth vector curves, antialiasing, or glow haze. Perfectly flat `#ff00ff` chroma background; no magenta in the sprite.

### Targeted revision

Change only two details in the first Birdarang source: enlarge the single red technology light at the top so it remains visible after reduction to 32x32, and make the central vertical body slightly slimmer. Preserve the upright top-down orientation, crescent wings, downward-pointing tips, symmetry, palette, padding, and all other constraints.

Final cleanup: the reduced source left the lamp as a detached one-pixel mark that disappeared at 16x16. The production texture replaces it with a connected 2x2 red lamp; this is visible as one red pixel at the 16x16 readability check.
