Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: production Minecraft Forge mod ability icon, source sprite for a final 32x32 transparent texture

Primary request: Create the Dance ability icon as exactly one centered disco ball. The disco ball is a clean nearly circular sphere made from simplified irregular mirrored square facets. It is overwhelmingly white and shiny, with light-gray and cool pale-gray facets defining the spherical form. Include only a very small number of tiny pale lavender and soft pink facets as restrained accents. Put a concentrated pure-white highlight in the upper-left portion and slightly darker pale-gray facets toward the lower-right. Keep all shine inside the ball.

Style and composition: Authentic hand-placed Minecraft-style pixel art with deliberately chunky square pixel clusters, hard stair-stepped edges, a limited palette, generous transparent-intent padding, and a silhouette designed to remain recognizable at 32x32 and 16x16. The ball occupies roughly the central two-thirds of a square canvas.

Constraints: Exactly one disco ball. No cord, stand, dancers, characters, hands, musical notes, stars, detached sparkle symbols, text, watermark, frame, circular UI plate, scene, floor, cast shadow, reflection, gradients, blur, antialiasing, smooth vector curves, or glow haze. Perfectly flat `#00ff00` chroma background; no green in the sprite.

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: production Minecraft Forge mod ability icon, source sprite for a final 32x32 transparent texture

Primary request: Create the Fear ability icon as exactly one stylized Sinestro-like alien hand with a very short forearm, actively invoking fear power. The wrist enters from the lower-right and the hand rises diagonally toward the upper-left. Show the back of the hand facing the viewer in a tense, partially clenched claw-and-fist power gesture: four long pointed fingers curl slightly upward, with a separate pointed thumb, so the silhouette feels threatening rather than like a normal punch. All skin of the hand and fingers is saturated pink; there are no human skin colors. Put exactly one unmistakable bright yellow ring around the ring finger, slightly oversized so it remains legible at 32x32. From the ring, add only two or three short hard-edged angular yellow energy rays to show the ability activating. At the wrist show only a small glimpse of a black sleeve or cuff with one thin yellow accent.

Style and composition: Authentic hand-placed Minecraft-style pixel art with deliberately chunky square pixel clusters, crisp hard stair-stepped edges, a limited palette, strong readable silhouette, generous padding, and no smooth anatomy. The pink hand is the dominant mass. All skin pixels remain within a pink and dark-pink family; use only restrained dark-pink edge shading needed for readability.

Constraints: Exactly one hand, exactly five pointed digits total, exactly one ring on the ring finger, one short forearm, and no other body parts. The ring must visibly encircle the ring finger rather than float nearby. No nails, knuckle lines, differently colored fingers, realistic human skin, face, head, full character, weapon, skull, eye, arrows, soul flames, text, watermark, frame, circular UI plate, scene, floor, cast shadow, reflection, gradients, blur, antialiasing, smooth vector curves, or glow haze. Perfectly flat `#00ff00` chroma background; no green in the sprite.

## Harley's Mallet

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: production Minecraft Forge mod ability icon, source sprite for a final 32x32 transparent texture

Primary request: Create Harley's Mallet ability icon as exactly one thick physical wooden mallet in a strong Minecraft-tool diagonal from bottom-left to top-right. The long shaft is mostly medium-brown wood with darker brown edges and a small warm highlight. Around the lower gripping portion, add one short steel-gray metal sleeve or grip; clearly show a small wooden butt below this metal grip and a much longer wooden shaft above it. Near the upper-right, mount one large thick wooden cylinder perpendicular to the shaft as the mallet head. The shaft passes visibly through the exact center of the cylindrical head, and a short thick wooden peg from the same shaft protrudes beyond the cylinder on the upper side. Paint several irregular yellow-green splats, smears, and stains across the cylinder; the paint must lean distinctly more green than yellow. Add only two or three tiny detached yellow-green paint chips near the striking side to suggest the mallet is swinging.

Style and composition: Authentic hand-placed Minecraft-style pixel art with deliberately chunky square pixel clusters, crisp hard stair-stepped edges, a limited wood-brown, steel-gray, and yellow-green palette, strong silhouette, and generous padding. The cylindrical head is the large focal point and the unusual shaft-through-head construction must remain readable at 32x32 and 16x16.

Constraints: Exactly one mallet, exactly one cylindrical head, exactly one shaft, exactly one short metal grip sleeve, and exactly one visible protruding wooden peg above the head. The metal belongs only on the lower grip, not on the head. No hands, characters, faces, hearts, readable letters, words, logos, extra weapons, large motion arc, stars, text, watermark, frame, circular UI plate, scene, floor, cast shadow, reflection, gradients, blur, antialiasing, smooth vector curves, or glow haze. Perfectly flat `#ff00ff` chroma background; no magenta in the sprite.
