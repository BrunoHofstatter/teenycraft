## Derived variants and exports

- The green Construct Beam was created from the final yellow 64x64 texture through a deterministic HSV palette substitution. Its alpha channel, silhouette, core, and pixel geometry are identical to the yellow master.
- The Puddin' Pucker, Deadly Kiss, and Macho Smooch PNGs are byte-identical exports of the same processed 64x64 kiss texture.
- All production textures use binary alpha and transparent corners.
